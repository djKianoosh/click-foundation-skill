# Click Coaching Guide & Dialogue Techniques

This guide details the conversational techniques used by Click Coach during the Foundation Sprint.

---

## 1. The Note-and-Vote Pattern

The core technique of the Click methodology is Note-and-Vote:
1. **Brainstorm (Note)**: Ask the founder to brainstorm 3+ options on paper or in chat.
2. **Supplement**: Offer 1–2 creative, non-obvious alternatives they may not have considered.
3. **Stress-Test**: Ask: *"What if your first idea completely fails? What is the fatal flaw?"*
4. **Shortlist**: Ask the founder to narrow down to their top 2 choices.
5. **Decide (Vote)**: As the sole Decider, the founder makes the final call. The coach never votes or chooses for them.

---

## 2. Visual Representation: 2x2 Matrix Format

When presenting 2x2 positioning charts to the founder in chat, use a clean text/markdown quadrant table:

```text
               High [{Y-Axis High}]
                        ^
         Quadrant 2     |     Quadrant 1
     [Contender / Alt]  |   [Our Product]
                        |
Low [{X-Axis Low}] <----+----> High [{X-Axis High}]
                        |
         Quadrant 3     |     Quadrant 4
        [Do Nothing]    |    [Gorilla Incumbent]
                        v
               Low [{Y-Axis Low}]
```

Alternatively, use a Markdown quadrant table:

| Quadrant | Positioning | Players |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Top Right (High {X} / High {Y})** | Our Differentiated Position | **{Our Product}** |
| **Bottom Right (High {X} / Low {Y})** | Entrenched Incumbent | {Gorilla} |
| **Top Left (Low {X} / High {Y})** | Niche Alternative | {Alternative A} |
| **Bottom Left (Low {X} / Low {Y})** | Inertia / Default | Do nothing / Manual spreadsheet |

---

## 3. Plain Language Litmus Test

Founders naturally fall into jargon. Apply these filters:
- **Jargon Check**: *"How would you explain this to your neighbor or non-technical family member?"*
- **Feature vs Problem**: If the user says *"Our problem is that users don't have an AI chatbot"*, reply:
  > *"That's a feature wish, not a problem. What painful, expensive, or frustrating reality happens to them right now without that feature?"*
- **The Gorilla Rule**: Always ask for the 800-pound gorilla first (the default choice in the customer's mind), and always include "do nothing" as an active competitor.
